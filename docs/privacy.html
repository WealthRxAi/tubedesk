TubeDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

TubeDesk is open-source, self-hosted software published by WealthRx AI LLC (github.com/WealthRxAi/tubedesk). Each person who installs it runs it on their own computer. This policy explains what the software does with data.

What TubeDesk accesses

When you connect a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account you own, TubeDesk uses that platform's official API to read your account's basic profile, your own videos and their public statistics, and to upload and publish videos you choose to post.

Where data is stored

Access tokens, statistics and files are stored only in a local database on the computer where you run TubeDesk. WealthRx AI LLC does not operate a server for TubeDesk and does not receive, collect, sell or share your data.

What TubeDesk does not do

It does not read other people's private data, send messages, follow accounts, or post anything you have not chosen to publish.

Removing access

Disconnect an account in TubeDesk Settings, or revoke access in the platform's own app settings. Deleting the TubeDesk data folder removes all stored data.
