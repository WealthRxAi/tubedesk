Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy covers the hosted TubeDesk service at gettubedesk.com, including the TubeDesk connector for AI assistants, the browser extension and the mobile app. TubeDesk is operated by LLCreativity LLC ("we", "us"). If you run the open-source version of TubeDesk on your own computer, your data stays on that computer and we do not receive it.

What TubeDesk is

TubeDesk helps YouTube creators research video ideas, improve their videos' titles and descriptions, cut short clips from their own videos, and schedule posts. It can be used on our website or from inside an AI assistant you connect it to.

YouTube API Services

TubeDesk uses YouTube API Services to read and manage your YouTube channel on your behalf. By using TubeDesk you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture.

YouTube data you authorize. When you connect a YouTube channel, we access through YouTube API Services:

  • your channel's details and settings;
  • your videos and their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and privacy status;
  • comments on your videos;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for your channel, such as views, watch time, audience retention and traffic sources;
  • revenue reports, only if you separately choose to turn on the earnings features.

Public YouTube data. We collect publicly available information about videos and channels on YouTube, such as titles, thumbnails and view counts, to provide research features.

Other accounts you connect. If you connect an Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok or LinkedIn account, we receive that account's basic profile and the permission to publish the posts you ask us to publish.

Files you give us. Videos, images and scripts you upload or ask TubeDesk to process.

Requests and usage. The requests you make to TubeDesk, including those sent through an AI assistant, and basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request.

Payment information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your plan, payment status and the last four digits of your card. We do not receive or store your full card number.

How we use information

  • To provide the features you ask for: reports, ideas, research, edits to your videos, clips, scheduling and publishing.
  • To make changes to your YouTube channel or publish to your connected accounts, only when you ask for or approve the change.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and stay within the limits set by YouTube and other platforms.
  • To bill you and to send you service messages, such as alerts you have turned on.

We do not sell your information. We do not show advertising. We do not use your YouTube data or your content to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google API Services: Limited Use

TubeDesk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Artificial intelligence

Some features use AI models to produce ideas, titles, scripts, summaries and draft replies. To do this we send the relevant text, such as a video's title, transcript or comments, to an AI model provider that processes it on our behalf and is not permitted to use it to train its models. If you use TubeDesk through your own AI assistant, the results TubeDesk returns are also handled by that assistant's provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

Who we share information with

  • Service providers that host the service, store data, process payments, send email and run AI models for us, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing those services.
  • Platforms you connect. When you ask us to change a video or publish a post, we send that content to YouTube or the platform you chose.
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfer. If the service is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new operator, who must honor this policy.

How long we keep information

  • We keep your account information and the data from your connected channel for as long as your account is active and the connection is authorized.
  • Stored YouTube data is refreshed from YouTube at least every 30 days, or deleted.
  • If you disconnect a channel or revoke TubeDesk's access, we delete that channel's stored YouTube data and access tokens within 7 days.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your account information, connected-account tokens and uploaded files within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax, billing or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Disconnect or delete. You can disconnect any channel or account, or delete your TubeDesk account, in TubeDesk's settings.
  • Revoke Google access. You can remove TubeDesk's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Access and correction. You can ask us for a copy of your information or ask us to correct or delete it by writing to the address below.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar local storage that are necessary to keep you signed in and to remember your settings. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

Security

Access tokens for your connected accounts are encrypted when stored. Access to user data is limited to what is needed to run and support the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you as required by law if a breach affects your information.

Children

TubeDesk is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet the minimum age required to hold a YouTube account in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Where information is processed

We operate from the United States, and your information is processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate.
