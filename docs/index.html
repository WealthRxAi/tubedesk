Questions

Can I use it today?

The open-source version runs on a Mac today and is free. The hosted version, where you only sign in, is being built. Ask for early access and we will email you when it opens.

Is TubeDesk made by YouTube?

No. TubeDesk is an independent tool from LLCreativity LLC. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Do I need ChatGPT or Claude?

No. You can use TubeDesk on its own site. If you already use an AI assistant, you can also connect TubeDesk to it and ask there.

Will it post without asking me?

No. TubeDesk shows you each change or post and waits for your approval. If you turn on Autopilot, you choose what it may do on its own.

How do you estimate search numbers?

YouTube does not publish search volumes. TubeDesk estimates them from search suggestions and public video data, and labels them as estimates.
