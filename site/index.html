Other tools tell you what to do. TubeDesk does it.

TubeDesk helps YouTube creators grow their channels. It finds video ideas, fixes titles and descriptions, cuts Shorts from your videos and schedules your posts. It works inside the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT or Claude.

Ask for early access The hosted version of TubeDesk is being built and is not open to the public yet. Sign-up opens after Google completes its review of the app. The open-source version is available today.

What it does

What should I make next?

Video ideas scored against your own channel, keyword research, and videos that are outperforming in your niche.

Why isn't my channel growing?

A channel report with the biggest problems first. Each one has a Fix it button, and every change can be undone.

Do it for me

Shorts cut from your long videos, posts scheduled to your connected accounts, and comment replies drafted for your approval.

How it works

  • Connect TubeDesk to your AI. Add it in ChatGPT or Claude, or use the TubeDesk website.
  • Sign in with Google. You choose which YouTube channel to connect. There are no API keys or setup steps.
  • Ask in your own words. "Why did my last video underperform?" or "Give me five ideas for next week."
  • You stay in control. TubeDesk asks before it changes anything on your channel, and keeps an undo for every change.
